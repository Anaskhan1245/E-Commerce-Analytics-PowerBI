# 🛒 E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive E-Commerce Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI, Power Query and DAX to analyze sales performance, products, brands, sellers, pricing, inventory, delivery performance and customer-related metrics.

---

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce marketplace data to understand business performance across products, categories, brands, sellers and operational areas.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of revenue, units sold, product performance, seller performance, pricing and discount behavior, inventory availability, delivery performance, ratings, reviews and return-related metrics.

A dedicated Insights & Recommendations page was created to convert data findings into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and sales performance
- Identify top-performing categories
- Identify top-performing products and brands
- Analyze seller performance
- Analyze revenue by seller city
- Understand pricing and discount patterns
- Analyze discount impact on sales volume
- Monitor inventory availability
- Identify low-stock and slow-moving products
- Analyze delivery performance
- Evaluate product ratings and reviews
- Analyze returnable products and return policies
- Generate actionable business insights
- Provide data-driven business recommendations
